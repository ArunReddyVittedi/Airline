# Cancellation, changes and refunds

## Cancelling your own booking
The fee depends on how long before departure you cancel.

- More than 24 hours before departure: no fee. The full fare is refunded.
- Within 24 hours of departure: a fee of 25 percent of the fare is charged, and the rest is
  refunded.
- After departure: the fare is not refundable.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within five to seven working days.
Taxes and airport charges are always refunded in full, including on a non refundable fare.

## When the airline cancels
If we cancel the flight, the fare is refunded in full with no fee, whatever the notice
given, and whatever fare type was bought. This applies even inside the 24 hour window.

You may instead choose to be moved to another flight on the same route at no extra cost. If
the alternative costs more than the original fare, we pay the difference. If it costs less,
the difference is refunded to you.

## Delays
- Under two hours: no compensation.
- Two to four hours: a meal voucher, and the change fee is waived if you would rather travel
  another day.
- Over four hours: a full refund if you no longer wish to travel, or rebooking at no cost.
- Over six hours, or an overnight delay: hotel accommodation and airport transfers, in
  addition to the above.

Delays caused by weather, air traffic control or airport closure are outside our control.
Rebooking and refunds still apply in full, but meal and hotel provision does not.

## Changing a booking
- More than 24 hours before departure: a change fee of 1500 rupees, plus any difference in fare.
- Within 24 hours: a change fee of 2500 rupees, plus any difference in fare.
- Name changes are not permitted on any fare. A ticket is not transferable.

The change fee is waived entirely when the airline has cancelled or delayed the flight by
more than two hours.

## No show
Not travelling without cancelling first means the fare is lost and no refund is due. Taxes
are still refundable on request, so it is always worth cancelling even at the last minute.
