# Kashmir and Srinagar

Srinagar sits in a valley in the far north, and is known for Dal Lake, the houseboats on
it, the Mughal gardens and the mountains behind them.

## When to go
April to June is the best time for most visitors: gardens in bloom, 15 to 30 degrees, and
the mountain roads open.

December to February brings snow and temperatures below freezing. Gulmarg is a genuine ski
destination in these months. Flights are cancelled more often in winter than anywhere else
we fly, so leave a spare day either side of anything important.

September to November is cool and clear, and the least crowded.

## Where to stay
A houseboat on Dal Lake is the reason many people come, and is worth one or two nights
rather than a whole trip: they are beautiful and basic, and heating is limited in winter.

## Getting around
Srinagar airport, SXR, is 15 km from the city. Shared taxis are the normal way to reach
Gulmarg and Pahalgam, and both are two to three hours by road.

## What catches people out
Mobile data can be intermittent. Download maps and booking confirmations before arriving
rather than relying on a connection at the airport.

Winter flights out of SXR are delayed or cancelled by weather more often than by anything
else, and no compensation is due for weather delays beyond rebooking. Anyone flying home to
catch an international connection should not book it for the same day.
