# Check in and boarding

## When check in opens
Online check in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before departure.
Airport counters open three hours before departure and close 45 minutes before departure.

The counter closing time is firm. A passenger who arrives after it cannot be accepted even
if the flight has not yet boarded, because the baggage and security checks cannot be
completed in time.

## How early to arrive
- Domestic flights: two hours before departure.
- Delhi and Mumbai in the morning peak: two and a half hours.
- International flights: three hours.

## Boarding
The gate closes 25 minutes before departure. Boarding is by row, from the back of the
aircraft forward, and Business and GOLD passengers may board at any time.

## Seats
A seat is assigned free at check in. Choosing a specific seat in advance costs:

- Standard seat: 200 rupees.
- Extra legroom or exit row: 600 rupees.
- Front row: 800 rupees.

SILVER and GOLD members choose any standard seat free of charge. Exit row seats cannot be
occupied by children under 12, by passengers needing assistance, or by anyone travelling
with an infant.

## Travelling with children
Infants under two travel on a lap for 10 percent of the fare and have no baggage allowance
of their own, though a pushchair travels free. Children between two and 12 pay the full
fare and have the full allowance.

An unaccompanied minor service is available for children between five and 12, costs 2500
rupees, and must be arranged at least 24 hours before departure through the support desk.

## Special assistance
Wheelchair assistance, help boarding, and support for passengers who are visually or
hearing impaired are all free, and should be requested at least 48 hours before departure
so the airport can staff it.

Medical oxygen and stretcher travel need medical clearance and at least 72 hours notice.
A passenger who needs either of these should raise a support ticket rather than booking
online, because the clearance has to be reviewed by our medical team.
