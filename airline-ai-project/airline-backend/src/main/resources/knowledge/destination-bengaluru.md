# Bengaluru

Bengaluru is India's technology capital, sitting on a plateau high enough that the weather
is mild all year, which is the first thing every visitor notices and the main reason people
move there.

## When to go
Any month works. October to February is the most pleasant, at 16 to 28 degrees. June to
September brings evening showers rather than a real monsoon, and nothing closes.

## What it is known for
Parks, breweries, bookshops and food. Lalbagh and Cubbon Park in the centre, the palace,
and a restaurant scene that ranges from decades old dosa counters to anything else you can
name.

## Getting around
The airport, BLR, is 35 km north of the city and the drive takes 60 to 90 minutes, or
longer in the evening peak. The metro does not reach the airport, so allow for the road.

## What catches people out
Traffic. Two places that look close on a map can be an hour apart between 5 and 8 in the
evening, and a plan that involves crossing the city twice in a day will not survive
contact with reality. Group the days by neighbourhood instead.
