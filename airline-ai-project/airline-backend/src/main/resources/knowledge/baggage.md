# Baggage allowance

## Cabin baggage
Every passenger may carry one cabin bag and one personal item.

- Economy: one cabin bag up to 7 kg, maximum 55 x 35 x 25 cm.
- Premium Economy: one cabin bag up to 10 kg.
- Business: two cabin bags up to 10 kg each.
- The personal item must be a handbag, a laptop bag or a small backpack that fits under
  the seat in front. It is not weighed separately.

A cabin bag over the weight limit is moved to the hold at the gate. There is no charge for
this on a domestic flight, but the bag will not travel in the cabin.

## Checked baggage
- Economy: 15 kg included.
- Premium Economy: 25 kg included.
- Business: 35 kg included.

Excess baggage is charged at 500 rupees per kilogram, payable at check in. Buying the
allowance online before departure costs 350 rupees per kilogram, so it is always cheaper to
declare a heavy bag in advance.

A single checked bag may not weigh more than 32 kg, whatever allowance has been bought.
This is a handling limit and not a commercial one, so it cannot be waived. Split the
contents across two bags.

## Items that cannot travel
Power banks, spare lithium batteries and e-cigarettes must be in cabin baggage and never in
the hold. Aerosols, matches and lighters cannot travel at all.

Sports equipment, musical instruments and pushchairs are carried free of charge and do not
count against the checked allowance. They must be declared at check in.

## Delayed or damaged baggage
Report it at the arrivals baggage desk before leaving the airport, and keep the reference
number. A claim opened after leaving the airport is much harder to settle.

- Delayed baggage: 2000 rupees per day for essentials, for up to three days, against receipts.
- Damaged baggage: repair or replacement, assessed by the baggage desk.
- Baggage not found after 21 days is treated as lost, and compensation is up to 20000 rupees
  per passenger unless a higher value was declared and paid for at check in.

## Frequent flyer extras
- BLUE: no extra allowance.
- SILVER: 5 kg extra checked baggage.
- GOLD: 10 kg extra checked baggage, and one extra cabin bag.
